Privacy Policy for Scroller

Effective date: September 17, 2026
App: Scroller (Android package app.scroller.banner)

Scroller is a full-screen banner app that displays text you enter on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Summary

Scroller does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no backend servers operated by us for this app.

Information stored on your device

Scroller may save preferences locally on your phone so the app works the way you left it, including:

  • Message lines / presets you create
  • Color, font, speed, size, and related display settings
  • Feature toggles such as wand mode, max brightness, and related options

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

Sensors

If you enable wand / tracer mode, Scroller may use on-device motion sensors (such as gyroscope or accelerometer) only to adjust scroll speed while you wave the phone. Sensor readings are processed on your device in real time and are not stored for profiling or sent off the device.

Internet access

Scroller is designed to work offline. It does not require a network connection for core features and does not transmit your messages or settings to our servers (we do not operate such servers for this app).

Children

Scroller is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear the app’s local data or uninstall Scroller at any time using your device settings. That removes locally stored preferences and presets.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions about Scroller, contact the developer via the email address listed on the Google Play store listing for Scroller.
